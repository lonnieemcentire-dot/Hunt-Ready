Workout Breakdown
🌱
Phase 1 — Rebuild
Weeks 1–4 · 3 days/week · 45–55 min
Light loads, machines preferred. Re-establish motor patterns. Base cardio.

Day A: Leg press · seated curl · leg ext · calf raise · glute bridge + 15 min incline walk

Day B: Chest press machine · cable row · lat pulldown · pec deck · curls · pushdowns + dead bug / bird dog

Day C: 3-round circuit — incline walk · BW squat · cable row · plank
⚡
Phase 2 — Build
Weeks 5–10 · 3–4 days/week · 50–60 min
Increase loads. Add single-leg work. Cardio to 20–25 min sustained.

Day A: Leg press · walking lunge · single-leg press · leg curl · single-leg calf + 18 min incline walk

Day B: Incline DB press · lat pulldown · cable row · face pull · hammer curl · rope pushdown + Pallof / plank

Day C: 45 min cardio — incline walk + elliptical + rower

Day D (opt): 4-round circuit + bike intervals
🎯
Phase 3 — Hunt Prep
Weeks 11–16 · 3–4 days/week · 55–60 min
Pack simulation. Heavy single-leg. Long cardio. Shoulder endurance for bow draw.

Day A: Heavy leg press · Bulgarian split squat · loaded step-up · weighted glute bridge · calf + 20 min pack walk (15–20 lb)

Day B: Heavy pulldown + row · face pull 4×20 · incline DB · single-arm row · external rotation + Pallof / plank

Day C: 50 min — incline walk 8–12% + elliptical + rower

Day D (opt): Hunt sim circuit — pack walk · step-up · cable row · Pallof + bike intervals
⛔ Movement Restrictions
No overhead pressing of any kind
No lat pulldown behind neck
No barbell back squat (axial loading)
No deadlifts — until surgeon clears
Stop any exercise causing radiating arm/hand symptoms
No bow draw until surgeon explicitly clears you
Neutral neck at all times — no chin-tuck or craning under load
Face pulls/rows: retract scapulae, don't shrug
Treadmill incline: light rail contact for balance only
Watch for ring/pinky tingling (ulnar nerve — stop)
7–8 hrs sleep — non-negotiable for fusion healing
0.8–1g protein per lb bodyweight daily
