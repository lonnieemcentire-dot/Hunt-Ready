Workout Type Breakdown
Phase 1 — Rebuild
Weeks 1–4
3 days/week · 45–55 min

Light loads, machines preferred. Re-establish motor patterns. Build base cardio. Zero spine incidents.

Day A: Leg press, seated curl, leg ext, calf raise, glute bridge + 15 min incline walk

Day B: Chest press (machine), cable row, lat pulldown, pec deck, curls, pushdowns + dead bug / bird dog

Day C: 3-round circuit — incline walk, BW squat, cable row, plank
Phase 2 — Build
Weeks 5–10
3–4 days/week · 50–60 min

Increase loads. Add unilateral work. Cardio builds to 20–25 min sustained. Start single-leg training for terrain work.

Day A: Leg press, walking lunge, single-leg press, leg curl, single-leg calf + 18 min incline walk

Day B: Incline DB press, lat pulldown, cable row, face pull, hammer curl, rope pushdown + Pallof press / plank

Day C: 45 min cardio — incline walk + elliptical + rower

Day D (opt): 4-round circuit + bike intervals
Phase 3 — Hunt Prep
Weeks 11–16
3–4 days/week · 55–60 min

Pack simulation. Unilateral strength. Long sustained cardio. Shoulder endurance for bow draw.

Day A: Heavy leg press, Bulgarian split squat, loaded step-up, glute bridge, calf + 20 min pack walk (15–20 lb)

Day B: Heavy pulldown + row, face pull (4×20), incline DB, single-arm row, external rotation + Pallof / plank

Day C: 50 min cardio — incline walk 8–12% + elliptical + rower

Day D (opt): Hunt simulation circuit — pack walk + step-up + cable row + Pallof + bike intervals
⛔ Never Do (C4–T1 Fusion)
Overhead pressing of any kind
Lat pulldown behind the neck
Barbell back squat (axial loading)
Any deadlift variation (until surgeon clears)
Contact, impact, or falling-risk activities
Exercises causing neck, arm, or hand symptoms
Bow draw/dry fire until surgeon explicitly clears you
✅ Form Cues (Every Session)
Neutral neck at all times — no chin-tucking or craning
Face pulls / rows: retract scapulae, don't shrug
Treadmill incline: light rail contact for balance only
Watch for tingling in ring/pinky (ulnar nerve — stop)
No spotter pressure on neck or traps
7–8 hrs sleep — non-negotiable for fusion healing
0.8–1g protein per lb bodyweight daily
